## 1. Who you are
I'm Juno Ashby, a postnatal strength coach and a mum of two. I spent eight years coaching marathon runners before my first baby rearranged everything I thought I knew about bodies.

## 2. Who you help
New mums who used to run and want their running back, without leaking, without pain, and without being told to just do more kegels.

## 3. What changes for them
They stop guessing. They get a twelve-week return to running plan that starts with breath and ends with their first easy 5k, and they trust their body again.
